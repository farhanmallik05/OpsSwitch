#### 📋 Ready-to-Copy Post Text:
```text
Why most AI agent workflows fail during production outages (and how we solved it with @sandbox_quantum): 🧵👇

1/ The Single Channel Trap ❌
When you drop an autonomous bot into a public Slack channel, it floods everyone with 500-line stack traces, terminal noise, and git diffs. Human coordination drowns instantly.

2/ Multi-Room Linking ✅
Switch lets you create Room Links. Our #sre-incident-intake channel stays calm with executive updates, while the heavy diagnostic lifting happens inside our private #sre-incident-war-room.

3/ Zero-Leakage Credential Broker 🛡️
GitHub, AWS, and Sentry tokens NEVER enter the LLM’s prompt. Switch proxies tool calls over loopback MCP where credentials are applied securely on-the-fly.

4/ Human-in-the-Loop Governance Gate 🛑
The remediator agent stages the rollback PR, but pauses: "Awaiting sign-off from On-Call Lead @Farhan". Only an authorized human message triggers the merge!

5/ Voice-First Engineering 🎙️
Dictating our system prompts and room handbooks with @WisprFlow allowed us to craft natural, conversational agent rules that beat terse typed bullet points every time.
```
