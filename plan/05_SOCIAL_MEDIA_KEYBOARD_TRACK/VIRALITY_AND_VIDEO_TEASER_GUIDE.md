# Social Media Track: Video Teaser & Virality Guide

To win one of the 5 **Logitech Mechanical Keyboards**, your social post needs high engagement (retweets, likes, and comments from judges and fellow participants). Follow these production rules for the video teaser.

---

## 1. The 30-Second Video Formula

Judges and developers scrolling X or LinkedIn give a video roughly **3 seconds** before scrolling past. Structure your 30-second clip aggressively:

| Time | Scene | On-Screen Caption |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 - 0:04** | Fast zoom on Slack: alert pops up with red emoji `🚨 HTTP 500 on /checkout` | *"When production breaks at 2 AM..."* |
| **0:04 - 0:10** | Triager agent replies in Slack and cross-posts to War Room | *"AI Triager classifies SEV-1 outage"* |
| **0:10 - 0:18** | Split screen: Investigator runs `python repro.py` in terminal, outputs NPE | *"Investigator isolates commit & proves repro"* |
| **0:18 - 0:24** | Remediator asks: *"Awaiting sign-off"*. You type *"Approved"*. | *"Human signs off with one line"* |
| **0:24 - 0:30** | PR merges, healthcheck turns green 200 OK. Switch logo pops up. | *"Outage fixed in 8 minutes with Switch"* |

---

## 2. Technical Recording Settings

- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 for X/Twitter landscape (1920×1080) or 1:1 square (1080×1080) for LinkedIn.
- **Duration:** Exactly 28–32 seconds.
- **Captions:** Always burn in bold yellow/white animated subtitles (85% of mobile users watch without audio).
- **Audio:** Use a subtle, upbeat electronic background track underneath clear voiceover.
- **File Size:** Under 25MB so it buffers instantly on mobile feeds.

## 3. Social Engagement Tactics

1. **Tag All Relevant Hosts:**
   - Mention `@sandbox_quantum`, `@wemakedevs`, `@DevToolsAcademy`, and `@WisprFlow` in the main body.
2. **First Comment Strategy:**
   - Immediately post a follow-up comment linking to your GitHub repository and DEV.to blog post so algorithms don't penalize your main tweet for having external links.
3. **Engage with Fellow Participants:**
   - Search the `#HowISwitch` hashtag on X and leave thoughtful comments on other contestants' posts; many will reciprocate and boost your visibility.
